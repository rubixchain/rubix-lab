# The case book

Generated by `case_book.py` from each case module's `PURPOSE` and `STEPS` -
edit those, not this file.

Every case finds a limit of **the software** - Rubix - and checks that at that
limit Rubix refuses **gently**: with a stated reason, in good time, leaving
nothing behind. A case is a list of steps; each step says what the lab does
and what Rubix must do, and is judged the same way (`steps.py`).

## How a step is judged

Before the step, its preconditions are checked - the lab's set-up, never
blamed on Rubix. Every participant node must be able to receive (its IPFS p2p
listener; nodes that cannot are left out of the whole run). Then the step
reads the participants' wallets and starts watching their machines over SSH
(CPU, disk wait, memory, swap, disk, node restarts, p2p listeners). It runs
the action, waits for every accepted transfer to reach its receiver's node,
gives locks time to clear, and reads the wallets again.

| Outcome | Meaning | The case |
|---|---|---|
| **PASS** | Rubix did what the step expects; every check after it holds | passes |
| **LIMIT** | Rubix could not take all of it and refused the rest **gently**: every refusal named its reason within the budget (60s unless the step says otherwise); nothing left Locked; no value moved that should not have; no counter drifted; no node restarted or went deaf; an ordinary operation went through again afterwards | passes, and records the limit |
| **DAMAGE** | Rubix failed or refused **ungently**: no usable reason (a dropped connection, a raw database error, a panic), too late or never, RBT/FT left Locked, a counter left wrong, a node restarted or went deaf, or no recovery | fails |
| **WRONG** | Rubix accepted what it should have refused, refused what it must accept, or the result is wrong: value created or lost, an accepted transfer that never reached its receiver, two winners of a one-token race | fails |
| **HARDWARE** | the step failed while a machine it ran on was out of CPU (95%), memory (5% left), swap (200 MB filled) or disk (1 GB left) - possibly the machine's limit, not Rubix's | inconclusive (reported as SKIP) |
| **NOT RUN** | a precondition did not hold | not run (SKIP) |

A **capacity** step (a burst, a ladder rung) may meet a limit: a gentle refusal
there is LIMIT. A step whose operations are all valid and must go through has no
such excuse: a refusal there is WRONG, with its reason. Refusals from a
sender's own tokens (a former quorum's unpledged token, a second-hand part
token - both known product bugs) or its clock are counted apart from the limit
a capacity step measures.

What every case keeps out of the way, so a limit found is Rubix's: senders on
other machines than their quorum where it matters, one sender per node before
any node sends twice, wallets of whole tokens the faucet paid, DIDs that were
quorums earlier kept out of the sender seat, every sending node read back to
sign with the step's quorum, quorums funded for every pledge the step can make,
wallets that must hold exactly one token checked to, and nodes that cannot
receive left out.

## AUDIT-START

The fleet's state before the run - nothing created or lost, no token owned twice - so every later step's findings are its own.

| Step | What the lab does | What Rubix must do |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** Read every node | read every pool node's database and the faucet pair's, one consistent snapshot each | every node readable (one that is not is the lab's problem: the audit is NOT RUN) |
| **S2** Quarantine problem tokens | lock out of use every RBT token in a stuck status, or Free with no chain history, then read again - the audit's only write | done; how many is reported |
| **S3** Nothing created or lost | add up every DID's Free, Locked, Committed, Pledged, QuorumPledged and BurntForFT RBT | equals the 400,000 RBT the faucet minted |
| **S4** One owner per token | look for a token Free under two DIDs | none - two would be a double spend |
| **S5** Known issues | list RBT parked in Locked, token_denom drift and negative counters | reported, not failed (known product bugs) |

## QCAP-01

How many transfers ONE quorum signs at the same moment - whole RBT, and a fractional amount that makes the quorum split its own tokens for every pledge - and whether, past that, Rubix refuses the rest gently and recovers.

| Step | What the lab does | What Rubix must do |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** Set-up check | Set the quorum up and fund it for the largest rung; point every sender's node at it and read the lists back; take senders from machines other than the quorum's | the quorum is set up and answers; every sending node signs with it |
| **S2** Whole-RBT rung (one per rung) | n senders, one per node across the machines, send 1 RBT each at the same instant through the one quorum - a ring, so each also receives one | all n accepted and each on its receiver's node; past the quorum's limit the rest refused with a stated reason within 60s, nothing left Locked, no node restarted |
| **S3** Recovery after the whole-RBT ladder | one ordinary 1 RBT transfer through the same quorum | goes through in normal time - the quorum signs again |
| **S4** Fractional rung (one per rung) | the same senders all send one fractional amount at the same instant, so the quorum splits its own tokens for every pledge at once | as S2; a refusal leaves no token Locked and the quorum's token_denom counter right |
| **S5** Recovery after the fractional ladder | one ordinary fractional transfer through the same quorum | goes through in normal time |

## QCAP-02

Whether a quorum's pledge boundary is exactly its Free holding: up to it a transfer is signed and the quorum pledges exactly the value; one unit over is refused for quorum liquidity, at once, with nothing on either side changed.

| Step | What the lab does | What Rubix must do |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** Set-up check | Set the five quorums up; give each sender a DID on its node holding no part token (a fraction is then split from its own whole token), funded for the value | every quorum set up; every sender wired to its quorum and funded |
| **S2** Pay at the boundary (one quorum per offset) | read the quorum's Free holding H, then pay H-0.1, H-0.001, H, H+0.001 or H+0.1 through it, one transfer | up to H: accepted, and the quorum's Pledged rises by exactly the value; over H: refused for insufficient quorum liquidity within 60s, the quorum's Free and the sender's wallet unchanged, nothing Locked |

## NFT-01

Whether NFTs keep one chain across nodes when owners, subscribers and new owners act on them at once - including two executes at the same instant - and whether a node that no longer holds an NFT is refused rather than allowed a second deploy.

| Step | What the lab does | What Rubix must do |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** Create and deploy, every owner at once | each owner creates and deploys two NFTs | accepted; each listed by its owner |
| **S2** Children | each owner mints 3 children under its first NFT | the parent lists 3 children |
| **S3** Owner executes | each owner executes its first NFT 3 times, one after another | each accepted; the owner's chain grows by 3 |
| **S4** A subscriber executes | a node of another machine subscribes, waits until its copy of the chain has caught up, then executes | subscribed, caught up within 60s, accepted; the chains equal on owner and subscriber |
| **S5** Two executes at the same instant | the owner and the subscriber execute the same NFT at the same instant | never a fork: one may be refused with a stated reason, or both land one after the other; the chains end equal on both nodes |
| **S6** New owners | each owner's second NFT changes owner --asset-hops times | each transfer accepted; the new owner lists it within 60s |
| **S7** A former owner sends it again | the first owner tries to transfer the NFT it passed on | refused with a stated reason; the last owner still holds it - never a second deploy (core/transaction_builder.go:369) |
| **S8** Reconcile | read children, chains and owners | children listed; owner's and subscriber's chains equal; each NFT with its last owner only |
